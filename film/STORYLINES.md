# Storylines

Henrik asked for the film without stops, so A was built. B and C are kept here so a later turn can switch.

## A. Someone leaves (built, recommended: one person's access is a story a stranger follows)

- Device: a cursor does the work. Form: one continuous take inside one app window; the camera pushes, pans and pulls back, never cuts.
- The viewer watches an owner see the company's apps, choose who may open each one, switch off Jonas Lie, then find his refused sign-in in the audit log. It feels like five seconds of real admin work.
- First two seconds: the Nordlys Analytics overview already on screen; "One sign-in for your company's apps." arrives word by word on the left while the camera starts towards the app tiles.
- Beats: hook (overview, apps) → choice (app policies) → action (switch off Jonas) → proof (audit: Account disabled) → end card (mark, Explore the live demo).
- Look: the product's own ground and ink, Schibsted Grotesk, real 2x screenshots; one red word because refusals are red in the product.
- Sound: none. It autoplays muted on the landing page.

## B. The refusal first

- Device: the result first. Form: an endless zoom.
- Opens on the red "Account disabled" row in the audit log, then zooms out through the People row that caused it and out again to the app policies.
- Beats: refusal → who → why → the switch → end card.

## C. Two companies, one product

- Device: split screen to one. Form: two windows side by side.
- Nordlys Analytics and a second company run side by side; the same click in one does nothing to the other, showing tenant isolation.
- Beats: two workspaces → same action → only one changes → end card.
- Needs a second seeded company, so it was not built.
